Scientific Accuracy Notes

The sounds are called borborygmi or bowel sounds. They happen as the muscles of the digestive tract contract and move food, liquid and gas through the intestines. Most abdominal sounds are normal.
  • MedlinePlus — Abdominal Sounds
    Explains that bowel sounds are produced as the intestines push food through the digestive tract.
  • Cleveland Clinic — Why Do I Get Hunger Pangs?
    Distinguishes hunger-related sensations from the movement of gas and fluids that produces rumbling sounds.
  • Harvard Health — Stomach Growling and Other Digestive Noise
    Explains why digestive sounds are not necessarily a sign of hunger.

Accuracy note: A growling sound alone does not reliably tell you whether you're hungry. These sounds can occur during normal digestion, whether your stomach is empty or full. If unusual bowel sounds come with persistent pain, vomiting, or ongoing bowel changes, seek medical advice.
