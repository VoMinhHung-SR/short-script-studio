Scientific notes & sources

1. What does it mean when a hand falls asleep? The medical term for the tingling, prickling or numb sensation is paresthesia. It commonly happens after staying in a position that puts pressure on a nerve or affects its blood supply.

2. What happens when you lean on your arm? When you put pressure on an arm for too long, nearby nerves can become compressed. This interferes with the normal transmission of signals between the affected area and your brain.

3. Why do you feel pins and needles? When the pressure is released, normal nerve function begins to recover. During this process, unusual tingling or prickling sensations can occur. This is why your hand may feel strange just as it starts waking up.

4. Is it always harmless? Occasional tingling after sleeping on your arm or leaning on it is usually temporary. However, frequent or persistent numbness can have other causes, including nerve injuries, nerve compression conditions or certain medical conditions.

Important accuracy note: Avoid claiming that pins and needles are caused only by blood rushing back into the hand. Temporary nerve compression and changes in nerve signaling are important parts of the explanation. Persistent or recurring symptoms should be medically evaluated.
